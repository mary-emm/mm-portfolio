Does household income affect turnout?

Introduction

This was actually a something I was thinking of while doing the recent Johor election prediction (but couldn’t realize due to work and time constraints).

All election data comes from ElectionData.MY, the public website of the Malaysian Election Corpus (MECo; Scientific Data, 2025). The site dedicates its data to the public domain (CC0).

All code used can be found in this GitHub repo. A short write up can also be here.

Why do I care about this question?

When I joined INVOKE, I knew very little about how politics worked, and I had the impression that most people my age didn’t care about it. That was humbling. I still however, keep my distance from politics itself, but I don’t think the goal is to immerse yourself in it. It’s to understand it well enough to make decisions that are reasoned, not just emotional. I treat this exploration as factual answers to my questions that I otherwise won’t have known unless I did this.
